A glimpse into the future of sports cars — shaped by our heritage. With its timeless, purist design, mid-engine architecture, and focus on the driver and the driving experience, the Mission S bridges the gap between the future and our heritage.

Authentic.

Stripped down to the essentials: the Mission S showcases what makes a true sports car and delivers an immediate driving experience.

Consequent.

Every detail is based on an idea. Every element serves a clear purpose. Nothing distracts the driver; everything enhances the experience.

Timeless.

Inspired by icons: the design language blends the past and the future into a distinctive design imbued with Porsche DNA.

02 / Driver experience

FOCUS ON
WHAT MATTERS.

Every idea behind the Mission S stems from a single question: what does the sports car of tomorrow really need? The answer is a consequent focus on the driver's experience — from the five round instruments with an analog tachometer to the center dials with push-pull functionality.

TIMELESS.

A deliberate return to the core values of sports car engineering. Clear information, intuitive controls, and a precise layout of all elements ensure that attention remains where it belongs: on the road.

Analog at heart.

“On your own” mode.
